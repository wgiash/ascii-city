# A playable ASCII city

A living ASCII metropolis in a single HTML file. No libraries, no build, no
assets: JavaScript, a canvas, and characters. Open `index.html` in a browser.

It runs on the portfolio at [wayeezgiash.co/ascii-city](https://www.wayeezgiash.co/ascii-city),
where a thumb pad and on-screen buttons are laid over it for phones.

## What's in it

- A raycast 3D renderer that draws walls, rooftops, ground and water as colored
  monospace glyphs, with per-column depth and painter's occlusion
- An endless wrapping city: streets and avenues, an elevated beltway, a river
  with promenades, piers and suspension bridges, neon signs and billboards
- A reactor megastructure with intakes, pylons, smokestacks and a rotating
  searchlight
- Traffic that holds at signals, buses, boats, planes and helicopters, and
  people on the sidewalks and crosswalks
- A night sky with drifting clouds, stars and five planets
- Sky monsters that spawn over the city and come for you; a nose laser to
  hunt them with, and a health bar that says how that went
- Two ways to move: flying between the towers, or on foot with gravity once
  you land, where Space jumps and water gives no footing

## Controls

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W A S D | Move |
| Mouse or touch drag, trackpad two-finger swipe | Look and aim |
| ← → | Steer (recentres the aim) |
| R or Space | Ascend; jump when on foot |
| Ctrl | Descend |
| Shift | Boost |
| F | Fire the nose laser |

An earlier build with web-swinging, a walk-mode toggle and a radar lives in
this repo's history.
